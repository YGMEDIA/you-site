How it works

Tell. Arrive. Live.

The AI works once, during onboarding. After that YOU runs deterministically: what you track, you see immediately, without anything judging you in the background.

01 · TELL

The onboarding asks the questions other apps do not ask.

Personality, values, shadow, drive, goals and patterns, told in phases with progress and transitions. With the YOU Band your body listens in: where your pulse reacts more strongly, YOU keeps it as an invitation for a second look.

02 · ARRIVE

Your answers become your Digital Self.

The AI builds your profile once, with several perspectives, and sets up plans for training, nutrition and habits. The moment it comes to life is the first highlight of the app, not a loading bar.

03 · LIVE

Today is the command centre, the Data tab is the insight.

Rings, check-in, journal, habits, training, meals and weight logged right on the card, one tap instead of three. Score, trends, nights and recovery show what comes of it.

Today

One look, and you know where the day stands.

The four rings on top, below them the cards of the day: check-in, journal, habits, training, meals, weight and sleep. Every card shows the state, takes the quick log directly and leads deeper when you want it.

  • Rings for body, mind, consistency and recovery
  • Log on the card, no tab jump
  • Every card in its area colour, glass with depth
Digital Self

The mirror that also shows what you do not say.

From the onboarding come perspectives on personality, values, shadow, drive and patterns, plus connections such as integrity and fit. The AI does not judge, it reflects: strengths stand as clearly as tensions.

  • Big Five, personality colours, values and goals
  • Connections drawn from several answer fields
  • Neutral, balanced, ending with what you can do
Data

Score, trends and the whole night.

The Data tab turns tracking into insight: YOU Score from baseline and momentum, weekly dots, trend cards, sleep as a hypnogram with wake moments and a recovery instrument that stays calm instead of warning.

  • Time range filter from a week to a quarter
  • Habits, training and patterns with calendar bands
  • Counting animations and arc gauges instead of bare numbers
YOU Band

The band measures. The app understands.

Without the band you get to know your mind. With the band you learn how your body answers: heart rate, sleep phases, HRV, stress and recovery around the clock, automatically detected workouts, and during onboarding the Body Echo, where your body speaks up.

  • 30 to 40 days of battery, pairing via Bluetooth
  • Whoever owns the band gets the whole band experience without a subscription
  • No band never means an empty screen, but a calm manual alternative
Nutrition

Photograph instead of typing.

Photograph a meal, the AI estimates calories and nutrients. Plus barcode scan, food search and a meal plan you tick off. Three scans are free, YOU Pro lifts the limit.

  • Photo scan with calories, protein, carbs and fat
  • Barcode and search in the food database
  • Water and supplements with one tap
Patterns and habits

What repeats becomes visible.

Impulses and habits run as calendar bands across weeks and months. Every day can be tapped: status, time, trigger, note. So you see not only whether you keep going, but when it gets hard.

  • Streaks, crosses and ticks in the calendar
  • Time range filter for week, month, three months
  • Moment overlay with a celebration when a ring closes
Who it is for

For people who want more than counting steps.

YOU is not a fitness tracker with a notes feature. It is the attempt to capture the whole person, and it is considerate of those who are having a hard time right now.

People in transition

New job, breakup, fresh start. When everything is moving, a mirror that stays calm and shows what is really happening helps.

Athletes with a head

Training, recovery and sleep in view, and the question why the head does not follow on some days.

Founders and the self-employed

Energy, consistency and patterns as an early warning system, before the body sends the bill.

Everyone who wants to understand themselves

Not optimising at any cost, but looking closely. YOU shows strengths as clearly as shadows and lets you decide what to do with them.

Why YOU

Three stances that are not negotiable.

01

The psychological layer

The body alone does not explain why you stay in bed in the morning or cannot switch off at night. YOU measures body and mind together, because one without the other is only half the story.

02

Neutrality of the mirror

The AI does not judge, it reflects. A strong person with one fear sees both, not just the fear. Every evaluation ends with what you can do, not with a verdict.

03

Observation, not diagnosis

YOU never makes a medical or psychological diagnosis. A signal is an invitation to look closer. That protects you, and it keeps YOU out of the territory where an app has no business.
